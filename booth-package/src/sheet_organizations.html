Nervous system training for leaders and teams

Leaders and teams that hold their energy, clarity and connection under pressure.

Soma-Q is a workplace well-being partner delivering nervous system training for performance and productivity, with progress measured every week.

The problem we address

Under sustained pressure, the body treats a missed deadline like a physical threat. Left unaddressed, that activation quietly costs energy, clarity, sleep and judgment until burnout becomes the baseline for the people your organization depends on.

What changes

  • Leaders recognize what their nervous system is doing and shift it
  • Teams recover faster and stay present in hard conversations
  • Progress is visible every week, not assumed

How an engagement works

Leadership and team engagementsStructured, multi-week training for leaders and teams. The right fit for most organizations.
Presence Under Pressure™ workshopsA half-day session that gives a group a shared language and working practices.
Organizational partnershipOngoing support that builds capacity across the organization over time.

How progress is measured

Every engagement includes weekly measurement through the Soma-Q Leadership Progress Measurement System (SQ-LPMS). The SQ-M shows how the method is taking hold in each person, and the SQ-C shows the resulting shift in leadership competencies. Organizational outcomes such as focus, capacity and retention are designed to be supported by this work.
