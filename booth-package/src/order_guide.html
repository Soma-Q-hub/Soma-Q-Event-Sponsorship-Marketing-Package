Booth order guide

What to buy, where, what it costs, and when to order by. Event: Saturday, October 24, 2026. Prepared 7 October 2026.

Read this first. Prices and turnaround times below come from vendor web pages that my search returned, not from quotes. They change, several are "starting at" prices, and none of them has been confirmed against an order. I could not open any vendor's checkout. Before you pay, confirm the price, the production time, the shipping time and the cutoff hour on the vendor's own page or by phone, and ask for a proof.

Swag recommendation

My recommendation is one earned item, not a table of giveaways. The cards go to everyone who talks with you. A single premium item goes to anyone who scans, taps in, or books a call, so the keepable is attached to a lead rather than handed out freely. This is my judgment from the evidence in the strategy packet, where industry studies report that apparel, outerwear and drinkware are kept longest, while pens and similar items are kept for months.

Packing list

Banner and stand. Table cover. Counter sign in two copies with stand. Cards in a visible holder. One-sheets. Business cards. Earned keepable. Phone with POPL and Nimble, battery pack and a short charge cable. Tape, scissors and a small tool kit. A printed copy of the QR URL list to rescue a failed scan. Tally sheet or the lead tracker open on a tablet.

Before you pay for anything

CheckHow
The price, the production time and the shipping timeConfirm on the vendor's page or by phone. Ask whether the quoted time includes proofing and shipping.
Template and sizeDownload the vendor's template. If its size differs from the file, tell me the exact dimensions and I will rebuild.
ProofApprove a digital proof, and for color-critical items such as the Deep Teal banner, ask for a printed or hard proof if time allows.
Every QR codeScan from the proof, not from the screen.
PlaceholdersSearch each file for the character "[" so no placeholder prints.
