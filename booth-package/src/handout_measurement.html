Measurement at a glance

Most wellness programs report attendance. We ask: did the capacity change?

How Soma-Q makes your capacity under pressure visible, week by week, with the SQ-LPMS.

Participation counts and satisfaction surveys tell you who showed up and whether they liked it. They do not tell you whether you, or the people you lead, are recovering faster between demands, staying steadier in a hard conversation or following through under load. Soma-Q builds those capacities through training and practice, so it measures them too, every week, from the first session to the last.

What happens to your capacity to lead when pressure rises, and can that capacity be trained?

One system, two separate measures

The SQ-LPMS (Soma-Q Leadership Progress Measurement System) tracks progress over time. It does not grade people. It keeps two things apart on purpose, so a strong quarter of results cannot hide growing strain, and a slow start on results is not mistaken for a lack of capability.

THE CAUSE
SQ-M

Soma-Q Method Metrics. Is the method landing in the body and nervous system? Five capacity measures.

THE EFFECT
SQ-C

Soma-Q Leadership Competencies. Is the change showing up in how people lead? Five competency measures.

How the data is collected

BASELINESoma-Q Assessment at the start
WEEKLYSoma-Q Weekly Check-In, under five minutes
ROLLUPSSQ-M and SQ-C, trended separately
REPORTProgress in plain, usable language
ADJUSTFacilitation follows the data

Every item is rated from 0 to 10, and higher always means better, so a rising line is always good news and nothing is scored in reverse. The same questions are asked every week, which turns impressions into a trend line. Each participant receives their own Soma-Q Metrics Report in plain language.

Soma-Q is an education and training practice. It does not diagnose, treat or manage any condition, and no score is a medical or psychological measure.

What the reports show

  • Baseline and current SQ-M and SQ-C rollups, side by side and never merged into one score
  • The trend over the engagement, so you see the direction rather than a single snapshot
  • Practice consistency, for one person or across a team
  • Where capacity is strengthening and where it is still under load
  • Recommendations for keeping the gains, for you and for your team or company

For team and organizational engagements, what is reported at group level, and what stays private to each participant, is agreed in writing when the work is scoped.

What Soma-Q will and will not claim

  • Weekly scores are participants' own ratings of their experience. They are a record of change over time, not a medical or physiological measurement.
  • The SQ-LPMS is Soma-Q's own system and has not been independently validated. Reports describe what scores did, never what they prove.
  • Soma-Q does not promise a percentage reduction in burnout, turnover, absenteeism or healthcare cost, and does not claim to prove the cause of enterprise outcomes.
  • Every participant is different; not everyone will see the same change.
