Every program includes the Soma-Q Assessment, practices of five to twenty minutes a day, and session notes. Pricing is discussed on a discovery call. This work suits leaders who want practical tools and measurable progress and will commit to short daily practices. It complements, and does not replace, medical or mental health care.

How progress is measured

Before each session you complete the Soma-Q Weekly Check-In, a short set of questions scored from 0 to 10. Your Soma-Q Metrics Report shows how clearly you think, how steady you stay and how quickly you recover, week by week, so progress is something you can see.

How progress is measured

Every engagement starts with a baseline and tracks progress weekly through the SQ-LPMS (Soma-Q Leadership Progress Measurement System): the SQ-M shows how the method is taking hold, and the SQ-C shows how leadership competencies shift. Sponsors see where capacity is strengthening and where it is still constrained. Organizational outcomes such as focus, capacity and retention are designed to be supported by this work. Soma-Q does not promise a percentage drop in burnout, turnover or cost.

Who it serves. Executive and senior leadership teams, founder-led and high-growth companies, professional services firms, education and public service, healthcare organizations, and teams navigating change or sustained demand.
