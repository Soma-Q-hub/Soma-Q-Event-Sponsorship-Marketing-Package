## The insight behind it (from one session; a hypothesis, not data)

One session transcript is a single client, so treat everything below as a hypothesis to test at the booth and against other sessions, with permission.

1. The strength is the trap. A man who solves problems for a living brings the same engine to uncertainty in a relationship, and the engine keeps running when there is nothing to solve: analyzing, comparing, looking for what is wrong.
2. He often already has the awareness. He can name the pattern and dislikes it; insight has not changed what his body does in the moment. The work moves from understanding the event to noticing how the system carries it.
3. The wish is specific: to show up the way he intends, with a partner and with colleagues, and to "hang in there" without having to solve.
4. Underneath sits fear (of being alone, of the future, of losing control), which the mind treats as a problem to fix.
5. The tone that landed: respect for the capability, plain mechanics (locate it, describe it, ground it), shared humanity ("me too"), and a clear next action.

## Headline options (outcome first; test, do not assume)

1. Show up the way you mean to. (used on the proofs)
2. Stay steady at work and present at home, even when pressure is high.
3. Your mind is built to solve problems. Your nervous system shapes how you respond when there is nothing to solve.
4. What if the pressure is not a problem to fix?

Option 4 is the most distinctive and the riskiest: it can read as "stop thinking". Use it only as a second line, never as the main headline.

## Test plan (so the first booth produces evidence)

Nobody can say in advance which wording wins with this room. Print both counter signs and both take-home cards, tag each by piece (the build already tags pieces), and compare scans per piece afterward. Treat a small sample as a signal, not proof.

## Guardrails (from the brand rules)

- No em or en dashes; flowing sentences; American English.
- Never "you will", guarantees, hype, or "fix" as a promise. "Trying to fix it feeds the loop" describes a pattern; it does not say Soma-Q fixes anything.
- No energy or subtle-body language, and no "masculine" or "feminine" language, in anything public.
- Relationships: say "show up", never imply relationship counseling, couples work, or treatment. Soma-Q does not diagnose, treat or manage any condition.
- Credentials exactly as stated in the credentials source; Founder only.
- Testimonials need written permission. A male testimonial would help this track; ask before using any client's words.
